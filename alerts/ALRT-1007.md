# ALRT-1007 — Customer support transcript on a public wiki page

Sentinel DLP detected customer personal data on a DocPortal page in the `CustomerCare-Public` space, which is readable without authentication.

The page contains a full pasted chat transcript between a support agent and a customer, including the customer's real name, home address, order history and a complaint about a delivery driver.

The page was created by a support team lead as training material: "good example of how to de-escalate".
